## 7. Choices and withdrawal

**“Accept analytics”** and **“Reject”** have equal prominence. Closing the banner without acceptance leaves statistics disabled. **“Cookie preferences”**, available in the footer and on this page, reopens the choice. To withdraw earlier consent select “Reject”: collection is disabled and, if Analytics was loaded, the page reloads to stop its execution.

Withdrawal applies going forward and does not affect the lawfulness of earlier consent-based processing. It does not automatically delete already transmitted information; contact the controller for requests about that data. Choices apply to this browser and origin, not every device. If browser storage is blocked or cleared, choices may not persist and statistics still require fresh explicit acceptance. Material processing changes may require renewed consent.
